# Bundled command-line tools

The Windows package includes these separately maintained command-line tools:

- FFmpeg essentials build from <https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/>, used for
  audio decoding and encoding. See <https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html>.
- Rubber Band 3.3.0 from <https://breakfastquay.com/rubberband/>, used for time
  stretching and pitch shifting. The bundled executable is the GPL release.

Their inclusion does not change the ownership or licensing of those projects.
